Controls Keybinds:
(Default) indicates key is customizable in Settings - H.

Movement: W A S D (Default)
Attack: R (Default)
Talking: T (Default)
Pause game: B (Default)
Progress Talking/Start Game in Main Menu: Enter
Leave Customization of Keybinds in Main Menu/Unpause Key: Esc
Settings Key: H
Bullet Key: E


How To Play:

1. Nove to available tiles marked by "." on the traversal board. You will be warped to the Room board.
2. Fight or Interact with all enemies present in the room to proceed. 
3. Clear the traversal board to proceed to higher levels and escape psychosis.
